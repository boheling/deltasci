§01

Chatbots give you confident mush.

Free-form brainstorming produces plausible-sounding ideas with no grounding. "Be a scientist" prompts collapse two distinct expertises into one voice. DeltaScience keeps the domain mechanism and the ML feasibility as separate, arguing parties.

Free-form LLM

  • Mixes verified facts and vibes, untagged
  • Confabulates citations to fill gaps
  • "It might work!" — no measurable threshold
  • One generic voice doing everything
  • "I checked it" with no actual fetch

DeltaScience

  • Every claim tagged with type, source & coverage
  • Out-of-distribution material becomes a knowledge gap, not a fabrication
  • Synthesis refuses to emit without a falsifiable threshold
  • Domain scientist ⇄ ML engineer, in dialogue
  • Every identifier verified against the real record
§02

It catches the AI lying about its sources.

Every PMID, DOI, arXiv ID, GitHub repo and GEO accession in a claim is fetched and verified against the real PubMed / Crossref / OpenAlex / GitHub record. A fabricated identifier doesn't get a green checkmark — it gets a FAILED AUDIT that shows what the AI claimed next to what is actually there.

⚠ Failed Audit
08_audits / citations.json illustrative example
[CLAIM type=prior-evidence coverage=well-covered source="PMID 31452104"] Spatial transcriptomics predicts checkpoint-immunotherapy response in osteosarcoma. [/CLAIM]
What the model claimed

"Spatial transcriptomics predicts checkpoint-immunotherapy response in osteosarcoma."

Nat Med · 2021 · cited as supporting evidence

What's actually at PMID 31452104

A 2019 methods paper on bulk RNA-seq normalization — different topic, different year, different journal.

metadata mismatch · title ✗ · year ✗ · journal ✗

VERDICT — identifier resolves, but title / year / journal do not match the cited claim. Surfaced in the FAILED AUDIT section so a reviewer sees it before a grant committee does.

This is the BioIntel-class failure DeltaScience was built to stop: a fabricated PMID that sails through a naive "looks like a real ID" check. The adversarial challenger's own citations get audited the same way.

§03

Two minds. Three honest tags. As many rounds as it takes.

The dialogue alternates a domain expert (parameterized by a domain pack) with an ML engineer. Every line is tagged: CLAIM for cited fact, KNOWLEDGE_GAP for what only you can know, NOVEL_SYNTHESIS for a connection the AI is proposing, not citing. It doesn't stop at a fixed count; it runs until the hypothesis clears every gate. This run converged in four.

Domain ScientistRound 1 · framing

Non-response to checkpoint therapy in TFE3-fusion osteosarcoma tracks with an immune-excluded niche. CLAIM well-covered But the spatial wiring of that niche in this fusion subtype is KNOWLEDGE_GAP — it lives in unpublished pilot cohorts you'd have to bring.

Round 2 · engineeringML Engineer

Then model it as cell-neighborhood graphs from spatial transcriptomics; a graph attention net over niches has precedent. CLAIM well-covered Risk: your cohort is likely <40 slides, so we pretrain on a public atlas and fine-tune. CLAIM sparse

Domain ScientistRound 3 · refinement

Make the prediction falsifiable: niche-composition score must separate responders from non-responders at AUROC ≥ 0.75 in held-out patients. Tie attention weights back to known immune-exclusion markers. NOVEL_SYNTHESIS

Round 4 · convergedML Engineer

The gates pass, so we stop here. Six-step protocol, ranked risk register, and an adversarial challenger pass — every PMID, DOI and GEO accession above gets fetched and verified before this leaves the building.

→ hypothesis.md → protocol.md → risk_register.json → challenger findings → citation audit → full transcript

Six auditable artifacts per run, in one navigable directory. coverage ∈ {well-covered, sparse} — anything the AI judges uncovered is never allowed as a CLAIM.

§04

Three gates it won't talk past.

Synthesis is allowed to fail. That's the point — a refusal is more useful than a confident hallucination.

Gate 01

Falsifiability

No hypothesis ships without a measurable threshold — a number a real experiment could beat or miss. "It might work" is rejected.

enforced
Gate 02

Epistemic humility

A transcript with zero knowledge-gaps and zero novel-synthesis is itself flagged — total certainty is a hallucination signal, not a success.

enforced
Gate 03

Citation audit

Every identifier in a claim is fetched and checked against its real record. Metadata mismatches surface as a prominent FAILED AUDIT.

enforced
§05

Domain-aware by design.

A domain pack is two files — pack.toml + lens.md, ~50 lines — that parameterize the expert's reasoning lens. Ship with three, or author your own in an afternoon.

α

biomed

Mechanism, patient framing, evidence base, IRB / regulatory pathway, translational realism.

β

materials

First principles, composition / structure space, DFT bias, synthesizability, validation pathway.

γ

climate

Physical conservation, observational data ecosystem, statistical regime, decision relevance.

+

your pack

deltasci init-pack neuroscience → edit two files → validate-pack → run.
